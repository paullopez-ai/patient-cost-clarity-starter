# CPT Code Benefit Interpretation Guidelines

## CPT 99213 — Office/Outpatient Visit, Established Patient (Low Complexity)

Estimated allowed amount: $185. This is the most common office visit code for established patients with a low-complexity medical decision. Most PPO and HMO plans cover this under a flat copay ($20-$40) when the provider is in-network and the deductible is met. If the deductible is not met, the patient owes up to the allowed amount or remaining deductible, whichever is less. No prior authorization is typically required.

## CPT 99243 — Office Consultation, Moderate Complexity

Estimated allowed amount: $325. Used for specialist consultations requiring moderate medical decision-making. Many plans apply coinsurance (typically 20-30%) after the deductible rather than a flat copay for specialist visits. HMO plans may require a referral from the PCP. If the deductible is partially met, split the allowed amount between the deductible portion and the coinsurance portion.

## CPT 73721 — MRI Lower Extremity Joint Without Contrast

Estimated allowed amount: $1,800. High-cost diagnostic imaging that almost always requires prior authorization. In-network facility fees vary significantly ($800-$2,500). Apply deductible first, then coinsurance to the remainder. Outpatient imaging centers typically have lower facility fees than hospital-based imaging. Alert the patient to the high out-of-pocket exposure, especially on high-deductible plans.

## CPT 99395 — Preventive Medicine, Established Patient (18-39 years)

Estimated allowed amount: $250 (but patient owes $0). Under the ACA preventive care mandate, annual wellness exams are covered at 100% with no cost-sharing — no copay, no deductible, no coinsurance — regardless of plan type or deductible status. This applies only when the visit is coded purely as preventive. If any diagnostic codes are added during the visit, those services may be subject to normal cost-sharing.

## CPT 99284 — Emergency Department Visit, High Complexity

Estimated allowed amount: $2,450. ER visits are covered under emergency provisions regardless of network. Most plans charge a flat ER copay ($150-$500) that is waived if the patient is admitted to inpatient. After the ER copay, remaining charges apply to the deductible, then coinsurance. Total out-of-pocket can exceed $1,500 on high-deductible plans. No prior authorization is required for emergency services.

## CPT 90834 — Psychotherapy, 45 Minutes

Estimated allowed amount: $150. Behavioral health outpatient services must comply with Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act (MHPAEA) requirements. Plans with behavioral health carve-outs may have separate cost-sharing structures — often a flat copay ($30-$50) with no coinsurance. Check for annual session limits (typically 20-52 sessions per plan year). No prior authorization is usually required for outpatient therapy within the session limit.

## CPT 97110 — Therapeutic Exercises

Estimated allowed amount: $120. Physical therapy services are typically covered with a flat copay ($25-$50) per session when the deductible is met. Most plans impose annual visit limits (15-30 visits per plan year). Alert the patient when 80%+ of visits are used. If additional visits are needed beyond the limit, a medical necessity review and prior authorization may be required for a visit limit extension.
