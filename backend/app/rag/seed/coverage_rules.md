# Coverage Rules and Regulatory Guidelines

## ACA Preventive Care Mandate

The Affordable Care Act requires all non-grandfathered health plans to cover recommended preventive services at no cost to the patient. This includes:

- Annual wellness exams with no copay, deductible, or coinsurance
- USPSTF A and B rated preventive services
- ACIP recommended immunizations
- HRSA-supported preventive care for women and children

Important: The zero-cost requirement applies only when the service is coded as preventive. If a provider adds diagnostic codes during a preventive visit (e.g., ordering labs to investigate a symptom discovered during a wellness exam), those diagnostic services may be subject to normal cost-sharing.

## Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act (MHPAEA)

Behavioral health services must be covered at parity with medical/surgical benefits:

- Financial requirements (copays, deductibles, coinsurance) for behavioral health cannot be more restrictive than those for medical/surgical services
- Treatment limitations (visit limits, prior authorization requirements) must be comparable
- Plans with behavioral health carve-outs must still meet parity requirements
- Outpatient psychotherapy (CPT 90834, 90837) is typically covered with a flat copay when the plan uses copays for specialist office visits

## Emergency Care Coverage Requirements

Emergency services have special coverage rules:

- Emergency care must be covered regardless of whether the provider is in-network
- Prior authorization cannot be required for emergency services
- The ER copay is typically waived if the patient is admitted to inpatient care
- Out-of-network emergency services are covered at the in-network rate under the No Surprises Act
- Follow-up outpatient services after an ER visit may require authorization and are subject to normal network/cost-sharing rules

## Prior Authorization Flag Criteria

Flag prior authorization as required when:

- The procedure involves diagnostic imaging (MRI, CT, PET scans)
- The service is a surgical procedure or inpatient admission
- The visit exceeds the plan's annual visit limit for the service type
- The drug or treatment is on the plan's prior authorization list
- The service is performed by an out-of-network provider (some plans)

Prior authorization is NOT required for:
- Preventive care services
- Emergency department visits
- In-network PCP office visits
- Outpatient behavioral health visits within the annual session limit
